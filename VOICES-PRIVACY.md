# Privacy policy – Local Study AI Voices

Last updated: 2026-10-09 · Deutsche Fassung: [VOICES-DATENSCHUTZ](VOICES-DATENSCHUTZ)

**Provider:** edgebird-lab (Robin Olbricht – Olbricht Digital) · **Contact:** kontakt@olbricht-digital.de

“Local Study AI Voices” is a text-to-speech engine with built-in offline voices. It needs **no permissions**, has **no network access** and contains no advertising, tracking or analytics libraries. Text that another app (e.g. Local Study AI) has read aloud is converted to speech on the device and neither stored nor transmitted. The app only keeps the unpacked speech data (pronunciation dictionaries) in its private storage; uninstalling removes it. No personal data is processed or shared.

Free software (GPL-3.0 or later): https://github.com/edgebird-lab/local-study-ai-voices
